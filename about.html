About us

Built by a landlord, for landlords

Eni'L'Eni Rentful started as a simple rent ledger and grew into a full self-management toolkit for independent property owners.

Our story

From a paper ledger to a full portal

Most independent landlords still track rent the old way — a notebook, a stack of receipts, a mental list of who's paid and who hasn't. Eni'L'Eni Rentful digitizes that exact workflow, so nothing about it feels unfamiliar, only easier.

We built Rentful so a landlord managing two units or twenty can collect rent, message tenants, and see who's due — without hiring a property manager or learning enterprise software.
